How it works

From a phone recording to a decision an auditor can check

Four steps run on every interview. Each step keeps what it found, so the auditor can check the evidence behind every suggestion.

  1. 01

    Who spoke when

    The call is split into voices. The interviewer is the voice that reads the introduction; voices nobody can place stay visible as unattributed.

  2. 02

    What was said

    Each turn is transcribed once, in Hindi, as a whole utterance, and kept with its speaker and timestamp.

  3. 03

    Was it asked right

    The script is compared on the words that carry its meaning, so fillers, a respectful दीदी or a year said aloud do not count against the interviewer. Naming one answer option does.

  4. 04

    What the auditor gets

    A suggestion for each audit side, the quote behind it, and a plain reason whenever the system holds back.
